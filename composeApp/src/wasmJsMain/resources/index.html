Jason Toms

App developer and engineering manager, currently leading the DevX Tools team at Insulet.

I have been building mobile apps for a decade and working as an engineer for over twelve years. My work centres on Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and Kotlin Multiplatform, and on leading app teams. Outside of the code itself I spend a lot of time on how teams communicate, and I am a strong proponent of effective communication over rigid, overly formal process.
