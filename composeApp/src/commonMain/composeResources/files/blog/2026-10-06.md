Yesterday, at a political rally in Nebraska, Donnie J [suggested that Iran could take out Los Angeles
and San Diego](https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2026-10-05/trump-suggests-letting-iran-take-out-los-angeles-san-diego-at-campaign-rally).
People cheered for this. He is still the president today.

### What in the actual fuck is happening?
I am currently living in San Diego. Before moving here, Stella and I were half-jokingly playing
around on one of those [nuclear blast simulators](https://nukesimulation.com/?lat=32.71391&lon=-117.16633&weapon=b83#@32.74826,-117.22201,7411,343.7,-24.9)
to figure out where we should live. We decided to live in between Los Angeles and San Diego to
"maximize our chances" of staying safe.

So we knew this was something that could happen, but I think we even assumed we were just being
facetious and things wouldn't get that bad. Today I feel a little bit silly for thinking things
wouldn't go so far. But I am mostly furious.

It shows a complete failure of our government that these fascists have been able to take things this
far without being stopped. I hate to keep going down this line of thought, but just imagine if any
previous administration had done or said even a tiny fraction of the things this one is doing. 

I am too angry today to make a long post and to make a lot of sense, but I just wanted this
posted and timestamped for posterity. If San Diego is attacked, it probably wasn't a foreign power.
It was probably the people who know they are about to lose control. Anyone with eyes can see who
the real danger is these days, and if you can't, you might want to get familiar with George Orwell.

> The party told you to reject the evidence of your eyes and ears. It was their final, most essential command.
